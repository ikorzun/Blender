Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What you are buying

The ×5 Boost is digital content. It is delivered to the game the moment the payment succeeds — there is nothing to ship and nothing to return.

The 14-day withdrawal right, and what happens to it

In the EU you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content that is delivered immediately, that right ends once delivery has begun — and by buying the boost you ask for it to start at once and accept that the withdrawal right lapses with the first second of it.

When we refund anyway

Write to ikorzyn@gmail.com within 14 days of the payment and we refund in full if:

  • the payment went through but the boost never arrived;
  • the boost was broken by a fault on our side;
  • you bought by mistake and have not spent the minutes yet.

A boost that has already been played through is not refunded — the goods were used.

What to send

The e-mail address or the last four digits of the card used, the approximate date and time of the payment, and — if you can — the animal nickname the game gave you (it is shown in the menu and in the leaderboard). That is enough to find the payment; we do not need anything else.

How the money comes back

Refunds are issued through Stripe to the same card. Stripe sends them within a day; the bank usually shows the money in 5-10 business days. The refund is for the full amount paid, including VAT.

Purchases made elsewhere

If you bought inside the Playgama portal or in the mobile application, that platform took the payment and its own refund rules apply. Write to us anyway and we will point you to the right place.
