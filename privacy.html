Privacy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version

Blendo asks for nothing about you. No account, no name, no e-mail, no advertising trackers, no analytics, and no cookies at all. On the first launch your browser makes up a random player identifier and an animal nickname, and that is the whole of your identity here.

What stays in your browser

In the browser's own storage on your device: your level and progress, your collection, your balance, your purchases, the sound and music settings, and the random player identifier. It never leaves the device except in the two cases below. Clearing the site data erases it.

What reaches our server

The leaderboard. To place you in the table we store: the random identifier, the animal nickname, the number of the avatar picture, your score, the time of the last update and a signing key your device generated so that nobody else can write to your row. That is the whole record. We do not store IP addresses and we do not log requests with them.

The purchase. If you buy the boost, we store the payment identifier from Stripe together with the same random player identifier, so that the purchase can be restored on that device. No card data reaches us: the payment page belongs to Stripe.

Signing in with Google. Signing in is optional — the game is fully playable without it. If you do sign in, Google gives us a signed token; we check it and take three things from it: the stable account identifier Google issues for this game (a number, not your email), the display name on your Google account, and the web address of your profile picture. Your email address is never read and never stored. Of those three, only the identifier is kept on our server; the name and the picture's address are stored on your own device, and the picture itself is loaded straight from Google — without telling them which page you are on. If your account has no picture of its own, we show one of our animal portraits instead. The display name replaces the animal nickname on your leaderboard row, so it becomes visible to other players; your picture is not sent to the leaderboard and other players do not see it. What signing in buys you is that your purchases and your place in the table follow you to another device; your collection, your level and your boosts stay on the device they were earned on. Signing out on a shared device gives that device its own identity back. To delete the link, write to us at the address below. (Google's privacy policy.)

Both live on Cloudflare's infrastructure in the EU. Stripe is the payment processor (their privacy policy).

Music, video and the offline copy

The game may load its intro video from our own domain and installs a service worker so it can open without a network. Both are files; neither reports anything about you.

Other places the game runs

In the Playgama portal and in the mobile application the game is embedded by that platform, and the platform collects what its own policy says it collects. This page covers blendo.monster.

Removing your data

Write to ikorzyn@gmail.com with your animal nickname and we will delete your leaderboard row. Everything else lives on your device — clearing the site data removes it. If you had a purchase, tell us and we will delete its record too; the payment itself stays in Stripe's books for as long as accounting law requires.

Children

The game is suitable for any age and collects no personal data, so there is nothing to consent to.
